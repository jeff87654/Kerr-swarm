# Open questions: a ledger

*Reference from a working session, Nov 2026. Every open question the docs still list, in one place, gathered from each doc's Open questions (and the sketches' Questions it raises) as of the 2 Nov 2026 canon. A housekeeping pass on 2 Nov removed or replaced with pointers the questions other docs had answered, trimmed partly answered ones to what is still open, and tagged a few as deliberately open: story space to fill in fiction, not physics to settle. The question list below is generated from the docs by `site/open_questions.py`, so edit a doc's own list and rerun the script; the site build fails if this page is stale. ## Summary
- **The docs' own lists are the source.** Each doc keeps its open questions where they arise. This page gathers them by theme and counts them, and the build checks it against the docs.
- **The pass of 2 Nov 2026** checked 136 questions against the current docs. 18 were removed outright, where the same doc already answers them or the question no longer applies (released stripped gas, the settled 3 m² raft). 28 became one-line pointers to the doc and section that answer them. 21 were trimmed to the part still open. 16 were tagged deliberately open.

- **Seven proposed answers** below close or narrow questions with a short calculation. P3 finds that the cluster's white dwarfs likely supplied ~40–60% of today's metal, which would change matter-delivery.md's import figures.

## Proposed answers
Each is a proposal, not canon, with its numbers shown and its formulas in Method.

### P1. How often rafts pass (wander-year.md)
- **About once in three or four years, as wander-year.md assumes.** ~3 × 10¹³ rafts (governance.md) in a shell 0.9–1.25 pc out (~5.1 pc³) give ~7 × 10⁻⁴ rafts per cubic AU, one every ~11 AU. Passes within 2 AU come at n × π(2 AU)² × v: once every ~2–4 years at 150–250 km/s.
- **So the Passing is a festival of every few years,** and its swaps (people.md § Leaving) are a few days' window each time.

### P2. Leaked gas boils out (element-cycles.md)
- **Gas needs only its escape temperature,** ~1.5–2 × 10⁶ K for 250–300 km/s, not the X-rays' Compton temperature of ~3 × 10⁸ K. The Lantern's infrared glow pulls the equilibrium down to ~6–9 × 10⁶ K, still above escape. The X-ray leak heats gas that far in ~6,000–8,000 years, against ~10⁵ years for rafts to sweep it up.
- **So planning is right to price leaks as gone,** and the space between rafts holds a thin, hot wind blowing outward. Radiation pressure on its electrons is ~15–30% of gravity, which helps, but heating does the work.

### P3. The build-out used the cluster's white dwarfs (element-cycles.md)
- **The Shade's own had to go.** Near the centre the cluster held ~2 × 10⁴ white dwarfs per pc³, ~10⁵ in the Shade's volume. Each raft would pass one within ~20 AU every ~7–9 Myr, and each pass kicks a raft ~250 m/s, half its eccentricity allowance (0.003 of ~170 km/s).
- **The rest were the nearest ore,** decades away by light. The cluster's 2.5–4 × 10⁶ M☉ of carbon and oxygen is ~40–60% of today's 6.6 × 10⁶ M☉, so today's drums are likely that much white dwarf already, with little phosphorus or potassium surplus to bank.
- **That changes where the build-out's metal came from:** less from the disk's living stars than matter-delivery.md § How much assumes.

### P4. The hole barely wanders (full-swarm.md)
- **Random jostling is tiny.** ~3 × 10¹³ rafts of ~3 × 10⁻⁷ M☉ each move a 4.3 × 10⁶ M☉ hole at about √(m ÷ M) × 170 km/s ≈ 0.05 m/s, a drift of ~0.05 AU per orbit. If orbit families of ~200 rafts move as one, it's ~0.7 m/s and under ~1 AU. The gap between the Lantern and the Shade is ~17,000 AU.
- **So the Lantern follows the hole at centimetres to a metre a second.** What matters is deliberate lopsidedness, a bloc moving many families at once, which the rules of the road already govern.

### P5. A slow catch, booked ahead (raft-transport.md, ashgrove.md, sorrel.md)
- **Gentle means a long arm.** The rim curves away from the lane at 174.4² ÷ 3,050 ≈ 10 m/s². Today's one- or two-second onset takes ~30–40 m of arm travel. At the cabs' own comfort limits (jerk 1.5 m/s³, peaking at 1.2–1.5 g) a catch takes ~17–25 s and ~300–450 m.
- **So: one slow cradle at every station ring,** booked by the frail, the ill and parents with newborns. Tube cars end their trips with the same catch, so it serves long trips too. Hedda can travel; she books ahead.

### P6. Net the whole Spindle at night (sorrel.md)
- **The fall is slower than sorrel.md says, and still lethal.** Its ~85 m/s is the vacuum figure. In the Hollow's heliox (~0.4 kg/m³) drag holds a drifting flyer to ~30–40 m/s at the floor, after about three minutes.
- **There's time to catch them.** Felt weight passes 0.1 g about 300 m out, ~75 s after the drift begins. A light net ~150 m from the axis (0.05 g, still climbable) covers the full 3.75 km in ~3.5 km², ~100–200 t at 30–50 g/m², rolled out for the lamp-night and stowed by day.

### P7. Sails deploy in days (governance.md)
- **The film is big but easy to lay.** Outside the belt a person needs 3–22 m² of sail and 4–20 m² of radiator, ~1,400–8,000 km² of film for a drum of 200 M against its 600 km² cell. A hundred booms each laying a 10 m strip at 5 m/s (~18 km² an hour) lay it in ~3–19 days.
- **So even an unannounced Dark leaves 5–7 weeks of the 55-day reserve,** and laying the sails fits the monthly drills Sorrel's Reserve crews already run.

## Method
- **The pass.** A script read every doc's Open questions and Questions it raises, bullet by bullet (136 bullets in 23 docs on 2 Nov 2026). Each was checked by grepping its key terms across the README and the docs and reading the hits, with the README's canon order deciding disagreements. Answered questions became pointers ("**Topic:** answered in doc § Section") unless the doc itself already said so, in which case they were removed.
- **The generated list.** `site/open_questions.py` rebuilds the part of this page between its markers from the docs: a bullet that starts "**Topic:** answered" counts as answered, one that ends "(deliberately open)" as story space, the rest as open. `site/build.py` runs it with `--check` and stops if this page is stale.
