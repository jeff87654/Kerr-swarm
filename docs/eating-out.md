# Eating out: the Crosswise Guide to Herb Row

*In-world restaurant guide from a working session, Oct 2026. Where to eat along Herb Row in Merrow, written as the Herb Row Eating Society's guide. Builds on cuisine.md and food-system.md (tiers, heat rules and every dish price), economy.md (the price of an hour), daily-life.md (the wallet), raft-habitat.md (the Hollow, the Spindle, gravity by deck), ashgrove.md (Sorrel, Lovage, Ashgrove's Mill Hall) and open-drums.md (Chervil, Marit Vell's hall, guest-right). Jeff's decisions of 31 Oct 2026: the guide is canon; waste-heat cooking is a recognised style; low-g cooking works as described; animals are a rare luxury (one henhouse on the Row); osmotic produce exists as a recent Lovage invention; Tansy, Yarrow (a maker drum) and Borage (a fair drum) are on Herb Row. The places, dishes and the Eating Society are invented. Exploration page: [The Crosswise Guide](https://claude.ai/artifact/7uFqAF6nnY5tdpAZ1owYTa).*

## Summary
- **Every drum feeds everyone, so eating out is about choice, company and craft.** The commons' Mill table is free; a cooked meal out costs ~1–4 kWh a head, and the dearest dinner on the Row ~14 kWh, about two-thirds of a day's wallet.
- **You pay the kitchen in kWh and the cooks by the hour.** An hour costs ~3 kWh in Sorrel and Lovage and ~6 in Chervil (economy.md, open-drums.md), so labour is often the biggest line in a restaurant bill. A Mill-only tasting menu can cost more than a steak dinner.
- **Ratings are apple-seed stars.** An orchard apple cut crosswise shows a five-pointed star of seeds, the only star most people on Herb Row ever see (ashgrove.md). One star is worth the glide, two worth a cycler, three worth going dark for. The **Ladle** marks the best commons food.
- **Cooking styles follow the physics:**
  - *Waste-heat cooking.* The food plant's 65 °C loop is a free low oven, so holding, brewing and slow cooking cost nothing while searing and frying draw power (cuisine.md).
  - *Low-g cooking.* Soufflés rise taller and fall slower on third-g terraces; nothing boils at the Spindle's axis; tea poured from height bends spinward.
  - *Lidded plates.* Heliox pulls heat from food ~4× faster than air, so dishes come covered and hot pots are comfort food.
- **The guide lists 16 places in 7 drums**, plus food on the cyclers and at station rings.

## Sorrel
*Steady drum, 200 M, fixed goods by age. Gin near cost.*

### Ashgrove Mill Hall · Ladle · free
Ashgrove, off the Long Arcade. Nobody under thirty admits to eating here, and most of them do. Three Mill-table meals a day, served hot under lids at long tables; robots cook and the regulars run the room.
- **Lamp-dawn jook** of Mill jasmine rice in a sweet glycine-alanine stock that tastes faintly of scallop, with chili crisp, sour-starch puffs and milk tea (0.96 kWh).
- **The night stew:** red palm-style, with fried jelly cubes that drink it up and a stretchy starch swallow (1.50).
- **The bowl of the Nine,** which regulars work into a fat-sheened paste with chili oil and black vinegar to a ratio nobody else knows (0.17).
- *Tip:* the Hall gets the herb pot's cuttings first. Ask what was cut this morning.

### Keel Baths Kitchen · ✦✦ · ~3 kWh
The Quaysides by Station Ring 3. Everything cooks on the 65 °C waste-heat loop, and only one thing on each plate is seared. You eat in robes in rooms warmed by the same pipes, so the soup stays hot.
- **Onsen custard:** vat egg held at 63 °C for an hour until it barely sets, with koji tare and scallion (0.45).
- **Three-day broth noodles:** a gelatin-rich broth that has never boiled, gluten noodles, cultivated chicken fat (1.40).
- **Black garlic toast:** garlic aged five weeks on the loop until soft and sweet, on sourdough with cultured butter (0.55).
- **Warm amazake,** brewed overnight at 60 °C (0.20).
- *Tip:* wait for the one crisp thing. They fry it last.

### The Larder · ✦ · ~2.5 kWh
Deck 352, among the elders' flats near the Hollow. Knotnight all year: only food that keeps, the larder a habitat would eat if the long dark came.
- **The board:** three aged cheeses, cured mycelium ham cut thin as paper, miso-glazed curd, hard rusks (1.10).
- **Knotnight pie:** preserved berry purée under a lard-style crust, with cold custard (0.70).
- **Last year's larder sour,** lactic and pale, from the Court's Knotnight crocks (0.15).
- *Tip:* the second Tuesday the elders cook their blight-year dishes from memory.

### The Thiamine · ✦ · ~1 kWh a night
Pear Street, two corners from the Court. A gin cellar under the sign "Fortified, like our salt." Lovage people glide over on Saturdays, since their own spirits are taxed hard.
- **A Sorrel gin** over sugar-glass "ice" (0.18).
- **Krupuk and chili crisp,** with jelly cubes in salt and vinegar (0.30).
- *Tip:* at your fifth gin they cut you off politely and bring a free bowl of jook.

### Axis Tea Room · ✦ · ~0.4 kWh
Sorrel's Spindle, inside the netting at the flying halls, under 3% g. Bubbles barely rise here, so nothing boils: tea is brewed in the lift lobby and sent up in lidded bulbs. The house pour is from a metre off, and Coriolis bends the stream a hand's width spinward into the cup.
- **Long-pour jasmine tea** (0.15).
- **Netted dumplings,** crystal-wrapped, served in a string bag so they don't drift (0.25).
- *Tip:* crumbs float. Order the mochi.

## Lovage
*Steady drum, 200 M, auctions its fixed goods. Twelve hours of noon in the Hollow.*

### Noon · ✦✦✦ · ~14 kWh
A terrace on the back end wall at a third of a g, with the bright Hollow curving overhead and the sunline still at full noon at eight in the evening. Seven courses, nine cooks, forty seats, booked at auction.
- **A single cherry tomato,** warm from the lamp, eaten whole (0.10).
- **Orchard pearls:** millimetre cells of sugar, acid and aroma that swell taut and crack like grapes. Noon was the first to serve them (0.40).
- **Cultivated steak,** 200 g, basted in cultured butter (2.91).
- **Third-g soufflé,** on precision casein: it rises a hand higher and falls slower, so it still stands when it reaches you (0.80).
- **The apple:** one for every two guests, cut crosswise at the table, the star held up for the room (2.20 a head).
- The steak and the apple are over a third of the bill; most of the rest is the cooks' evening and the terrace's rent.
- *Tip:* Sorrelers call it showing off and come anyway, for weddings. Clap when the apple is cut.

### The Crock · ✦✦ · ~1.5 kWh
Low decks, near the Station Ring 1 lifts. The bakery behind the Lovage sourdough, which Chervil's halls serve and pretend they make. The four-century-old starter is registered with the strain library: an heirloom and a file.
- **The country loaf,** open crumb, crackling crust (1.10).
- **Croissant** laminated with cultured butter (0.55).
- **Saturday cheese-pull bread** (0.90).
- *Tip:* eat standing at the counter with the shift crews.

### The Glasshouse · ✦✦ · ~6 kWh
The Hollow's edge. A menu of the crunch nobody else can afford, the kind that comes from cells rather than the fryer. It rents a strip of the Hollow's floor at auction and grows under Lovage's long noon, paying Hollow rent instead of running lamps. You eat between the rows. Its cherry tomatoes are the ones Sorrel's Gate sees come through in crates (ashgrove.md).
- **Cucumber,** cold, cut thick, salted (0.45).
- **A radish with butter** (0.30).
- **Shiso hand roll** around sticky Mill rice and a smear of the Nine (0.20).
- **Strawberries** picked at nine, served at ten (0.90).
- *Tip:* Low-deck children get a free cucumber on their birthday.

## Chervil
*Open drum, 194 M, 3% Margin, guest-right (open-drums.md).*

### Marit Vell's Hall · ✦ · Ladle · ~3.5 kWh, free on guest-right
A block hall near Station Ring 2: four hundred seats at one long table, a paid cook and eleven volunteers, nine of them over seventy. You'll be seated next to someone. That's the point.
- **Thursday ramen:** hand-pulled noodles, gelatin-rich broth with koji tare, protein-flour chashu glossed with cultivated pork fat, a jammy ajitama (1.77).
- **Whatever the aunts made:** each volunteer brings one family dish (~0.5).
- **Chervil gin,** after six, in the halls only (0.18).
- *Tip:* on Booking Day the Quiet's savings become the feast.

### The Dawn Stand · ✦ · ~0.6 kWh
A coffee cart on the Hollow's back terraces, set up wherever the morning's painted dawn looks best. On a famous painter's morning the queue starts at five.
- **Cell-culture coffee** (0.15) and **honeycomb steamed cake,** yeast-raised and still warm (0.30).
- *Tip:* ask whose dawn it is. The painter is often in the queue.

## Tansy
*Steady drum, 200 M, one glide from Sorrel along Herb Row. Proud of its vat lines and its strawberries; keeps the longest strain library on the Row.*

### The Henhouse · ✦✦ · ~3 kWh
Forty hens on vat feed behind a glass wall, and the only shell eggs on Herb Row. Parents bring children to watch one cracked: a whole gold yolk is something most people in the Shade never see.
- **One fried egg** on sourdough, the yolk left to run (~1.6, food-system.md's hen's egg).
- **The two-egg birthday omelette** (~3.2).
- *Tip:* eggs sell out by ten. The hens lay when they like, and the Henhouse won't light them harder.

### Strain & Vintage · ✦ · ~2.5 kWh
Station Ring 5 Quaysides. A mycelium steakhouse with a list like a wine cellar's: every blight forces a vat line onto another organism family, so Tansy's blight years are vintages (cuisine.md).
- **The flight:** a mild *Fusarium* from a good year, the nutty *Neurospora* year, and a koji-aged cut with a crust like old cheese (1.30).
- **Frites and béarnaise** (0.70).
- *Tip:* the sommelier is a retired Gate worker. Ask what nearly got in.

## Rue
*Counted drum (people.md), two glides from Chervil, ~11 years from par.*

### The Blight Kitchen · ✦ · Ladle · ~1 kWh
Station Ring 1. Rue cooked its blight menu longer than anyone on the Row, after a *Fusarium* blight took two vat lines, and got very good at it. Young cooks run it, and it's full of people from across the rows.
- **Blight-cheese toastie:** coconut-style fat, starch and cheese aroma, griddled until it blisters (0.45).
- **Crackling "steak":** when the mycelium went, Rue fried for chew; puffed starch under chili and black vinegar (0.35).
- **Mock apple pie:** crackers, sugar, cream of tartar, cinnamon. It tastes of an apple you've never eaten (0.30).
- *Tip:* the cooks say they'll keep the menu when Rue reaches par.

## Yarrow and Borage
*Further along Herb Row: Yarrow is a maker drum, Borage a fair drum (open-drums.md's kinds).*

### Nine · ✦✦✦ · ~7 kWh
Yarrow's craft-school quarter. Twelve courses cooked only from the Mill (starch, sugar, fat, amino acids, ferments and a flavorist's rack). The ingredients cost under 1.5 kWh; you pay for the cooks. The sign over the pass reads "Don't brown your lysine."
- **"Scallop":** a glycine-alanine-proline glaze over warm sesame tofu (0.12).
- **The Nine, as nine:** one small paste per essential amino acid, each masked a different way (0.17).
- **Forty-layer kuih lapis,** a spice per layer (0.25).
- **Sugar glass** shattered over cold jelly (0.15).

### Lightfall · ✦✦ · ~5 kWh
Borage's station-ring arcade. Food barely travels between rafts, but recipes and culture genomes do, by laser (cuisine.md). Lightfall cooks only what has arrived, and dates each dish by its light: the raft it came from and how long ago it left. Cultures are regrown from their genomes in the strain library, about a week for a yeast.
- **Seven-year soup:** a sour fish-sauce-style broth from a raft across the Shade (0.90).
- **The passing-raft menu:** when another raft swings by, its recipes are cooked the same week, a fair held in light.
- **A cheese nobody on Merrow has tasted,** from a culture that crossed the Shade as a file (0.40).
- *Tip:* leave a recipe on the card. Lightfall sends one back for every one it cooks.

## Between drums
### The Wanderers' Galley · ✦ · ~0.5 kWh beyond the commons
Slow cyclers across Merrow. A 2 km/s cycler takes weeks to cross the raft, and its galley belongs to whoever rides, mostly twenty-year-olds on their wander year. The stockpot has not gone cold in living memory; each rider adds something from the last drum they slept in. A shelf of starter crocks, one per drum passed: take a spoon, leave a spoon.

### The Ring Counters · Ladle · free
Every station ring on Herb Row keeps a hatch for crews and commuters: a cup of Mill broth and a sticky rice ball (0.30). Sorrel's is peppery, Lovage's sweet, Chervil's comes with a sprig of whatever the hall cut that day. Arguing which is best is one of the Row's oldest sports. Drink it on the platform: glide cars don't take hot soup.

## Manners
- **Plates come lidded.** Lift the lid when it reaches you.
- **Rooms ring** in heliox. The best rooms hang felt; judge a restaurant by its ceiling.
- **An apple is never for one.** Cut it crosswise, show the star, share it.
- **Bring herbs, not gin.** A sprig from your shelf is the classic host gift; gin is too cheap to say anything (daily-life.md).
- **Salad is the treat and pastry the staple** (food-system.md's inverted scarcity).

## Method
- **Meal price** = dishes at cuisine.md's best case + hours × price of an hour ÷ covers. Noon: ~6 kWh of dishes (steak 2.91, half an apple 2.2, the rest ~1) + 9 cooks and front-of-house × ~6 h × ~3 kWh ÷ 40 covers ≈ 4–5 kWh + terrace rent ≈ ~14. Nine: ~1.5 kWh of dishes + a long, labour-heavy service ≈ ~7.
- **Waste-heat cooking.** The vats run at 60–70 °C and supply the kitchens' hot water (food-system.md). Onsen-style eggs set at ~63 °C; amazake and koji work best at ~55–60 °C; black garlic ages at ~60–75 °C; broths held below a simmer stay clear. All of it is holding, which cuisine.md counts as free.
- **Low g.** A foam's height is limited by its own weight, so at a third of a g a soufflé can stand taller and slump more slowly. Near the axis (under 3% g) buoyancy is weak, bubbles rise slowly and boiling is erratic, so water is boiled lower down. A poured stream moves in a straight line while the room turns under it, so it lands offset in the direction of spin.
- **Heliox** conducts heat ~4× better than air (food-system.md), so uncovered food cools several times faster.
- **Shell eggs:** food-system.md's hen's egg at 1.63 kWh (planning value; hens on vat feed at ~3–6%).
- **Orchard pearls** are cuisine.md's osmotic produce: regenerated-cellulose cells holding ~10% sugar, swollen to ~0.7 MPa.
- **Light-years:** the Shade spans ~0.9–1.25 pc from the hole (full-swarm.md), so a raft on the far side is up to ~8 light-years away.
