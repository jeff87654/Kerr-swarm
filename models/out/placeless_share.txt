7. TWINS
   One embryo a pregnancy: identical twins only, one pregnancy in 250, not heritable. At a second birth they are 3333 thirds a year,
   3.5% of the room, and a drum can charge them to it for good.
   Conceived the old way, fraternal twinning is a threshold trait expressed in mothers (liability heritability ~0.1-0.3; about 1% of
   pregnancies at the start). If a twin at a second birth is a third the drum accepts, such mothers leave three children where others
   leave two, and the liability's mean rises by (h2 / 2) x the selection differential each generation. Years until twins at second births are:
