A placeless-born life: the sums.

The drum: 200 M shares, 90 M places of 78 m2, mean life 120 yr,
  ~1.67 M births a year at par; shares of a steady population over 18 / 28 / 85 / 100: 85% / 77% / 30% / 18%
  born outside the allowance: 50,103 a year (one birth in 33); placed: 5,010 a year;
  placeless-born carried: 5.4 M, 2.6% of people (one in 38)
  of the placeless-born, children of the placeless-born (placing.py: 0.19% of births): 6%, one in 16

4. WAITING SHARES: shares unheld between a death and the birth they wait for
   At par shares equal people, so the ~3% of people who are shareless (children waiting ~6 years)
   are matched by as many shares waiting in other families: ~6 M (stem_places.py: 'held for the next child born there').
   Check: 60% of 1.67 M births wait ~6 years = 6.0 M.
   Their dividends: 5.4 GW. The placeless-born's whole commons: 5.4 M x 1.1 kW = 5.9 GW.
   If unheld dividends fall to the commons: (400 - 174.6) GW / 205.4 M = 1.097 kW a head,
     against 1.127 kW with no placeless-born to carry, and the 1.1 kW of a drum with every share held.
   A waiting wallet for every placeless-born person over 85: 1.61 M wallets, 27% of the waiting shares,
     1.45 GW: the commons at 1.090 kW a head (0.6% thinner)
   A waiting wallet for every placeless-born person over 100: 0.96 M wallets, 16% of the waiting shares,
     0.87 GW: the commons at 1.093 kW a head (0.4% thinner)

6. THE DOORS, A YEAR (an illustrative split of the placings; proposal)
   Room: 94,610 slots a year, half the households' to name and half the drum's to give.
   The households' half (47,305): 44,005 heirs named unborn, 3,300 placeless-born adults adopted (7% of heirless places).
   The drum's half (47,305): 1,500 placings for service, 45,805 permits drawn, of which 200 fall to couples across the line.
   Thirds by leave: 89,610. Placings: 5,000 of 50,103 born outside: one in 10.
     by adoption  one of a year's births outside in 15
     by service   one of a year's births outside in 33
     by marriage  one of a year's births outside in 251
   A crew that places 1,500 a year after 25 years holds ~37,500 hands on the placing track at once,
     and elects 1,500 a year from ~45,092 who will never be placed another way: one in 30.
   Draw Day: 45,805 permits at nine station rings, ~5,089 each; read out at five seconds a name: 7.1 hours.

7. MARRIAGE ACROSS THE LINE
   A household's lifetime chance of a third by leave: 11% (one in 8.8); by the draw alone, where the heirless
   name half the room: 5.7% (one in 18) over ~25 years of entering, ~0.23% a year (one in 440).
   What is given up, in householders: by a child outside with the door shut, 0.12; by marrying across the line unplaced, 2.
   A couple across the line entering the same draw for  4 years: 0.9% chance of a placing
   A couple across the line entering the same draw for 10 years: 2.3% chance of a placing
   A couple across the line entering the same draw for 32 years: 7.0% chance of a placing
   200 placings a year at those odds means ~87,917 such couples entered at once: ~2,747 a cohort if they enter
     for 32 years, 6% of a cohort's unplaced placeless-born.
