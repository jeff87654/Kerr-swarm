Where this is

Sorrel is a drum: a spinning cylinder three kilometres in radius and nearly four long, home to two hundred million people. It turns once every 110 seconds. Out is down. You weigh what you'd weigh on Earth at the rim and half that on the floor of the Hollow, the open middle, where the other side of the country hangs three kilometres overhead.

Because the floor is turning, nothing flies straight. Run spinward and you get heavier. Run antispinward and you float. A dropped cup lands a little antispinward of where it was aimed, and children say "anti", as though the drum had done it. Every game here is played with those rules, and with the real numbers.

  • Sorrel is one of 300 billion drums on the raft Merrow, in the Shade of a black hole's Lantern. It is part of the Kerr swarm world bible.
  • Every place in these games is on the Sorrel Atlas, a cutaway of the whole drum.
  • The Hours, their clubs and the Round come from the atlas. Going round the clock, the threads and each Hour's game are this page's own inventions.
