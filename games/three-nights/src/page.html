Three Nights
Anywhere

Merrow is a raft of three hundred billion spinning drums, each a nation of two hundred million people with its own rules, food and light. The next one is 140 seconds away by glide, and the glide is free. Any traveller may stay three nights in any drum that keeps guest-right. You have a year.

Every drum is generated from its place on the grid, so the same drum is always the same nation.
